🔐

AES-256 Encryption

Your data is secured using AES-256-CBC. It is transformed into a cryptographically locked blob instantly. We use PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations to derive a secure key from your password, ensuring even brute-force attempts are computationally exhaustive.

🌑

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

MojiMask operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge principle. Your raw image or password is never exposed. The generated "Emoji Key" is a clever localized encoding representing your encrypted data securely.

💥

Customizable Vanish Protocol

We enforce a strict Vanish Protocol designed to "Burn After Reading." You control the lifespan of your image by setting exact view limits (from 1 up to 1000 views). Once the limit is reached, the data self-destructs instantly and becomes unrecoverable.

🛡️

Browser-Side Privacy

All processing is handled directly by your local device's CPU. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or any form of data logging. Your privacy is not just a policy; it's hard-coded into the architecture of the tool.
